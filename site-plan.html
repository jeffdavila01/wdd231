Casil Shots Photography Site Plan

Jeffrey Davila | WDD 231

1. Site Name

Casil Shots Photography

This name identifies the photography business and clearly communicates the service it provides. The word “Shots” connects the brand with capturing photographs and memorable moments.

2. Site Purpose

The website will introduce Casil Shots Photography, showcase sample photographs, and help potential clients explore photography services for weddings, birthdays, and portraits. Visitors will be able to filter photographs by category, compare package details, and complete an inquiry form.

Target Audience

Individuals, couples, and families looking for photography services in Navotas City and nearby areas.

Planned Website Pages

  • Home: Business introduction, featured photographs, a brief overview of services, and links to the gallery and inquiry form.
  • Gallery: A collection of photographs that visitors can filter by weddings, birthdays, and portraits.
  • Services & Contact: Photography packages, service details, contact information, and a form for sending an inquiry.

3. Visitor Scenarios

  1. Where can I see examples of wedding and birthday photographs taken by Casil Shots?
  2. What photography packages are available, and what does each package include?
  3. How can I contact the photographer to ask about availability for my event?
  4. Can I view only portrait photographs without browsing the entire gallery?

4. Color Scheme

The website will use navy, warm brown, ivory, white, and dark slate. These colors will provide a consistent appearance while allowing the photographs to remain the main focus.

5. Typography

Georgia — Headings

Georgia will be used for page titles and section headings. Its serif appearance will give the photography website a classic style.

Capturing Moments That Matter

Arial — Body Text and Controls

Arial will be used for paragraphs, navigation, buttons, and form fields because it is easy to read on small and large screens.
